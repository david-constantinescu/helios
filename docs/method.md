# Method (for judges and reviewers)

1. **Compare people with themselves.** Each indicator learns a personal normal (median and robust spread of the 21
   days before the last 3). Changes are expressed as “× your normal day-to-day variation”.
2. **Persistence and trend, not single readings.** Watch needs 2 consecutive deviating days or a sustained trend; act
   needs a 3-day rolling median well outside normal.
3. **General ranges can only raise concern** (never reassure away a personal deviation), and a small set of
   hard rules (red-flag symptoms, extreme readings) always escalate.
4. **Confidence matters.** Learning, sparse or simulated data is low confidence; a low-confidence “act” is shown as
   “watch” with an explanation.
5. **Explain everything.** Every status carries sentences with numbers. No black boxes, no health score.
6. **Honest by design.** Simulated data, demo sensors and unreviewed guidance are labelled everywhere.
